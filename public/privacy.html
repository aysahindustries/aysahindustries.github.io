Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Applies to https://aysahindustries.github.io and the AYSAH INDUSTRIES Pi app

AYSAH INDUSTRIES LTD ("AYSAH", "we", "us") sells vehicles of every type, together with related services such as vehicle import and truck body assembly, and accepts payment in Pi. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and Pi app, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your full name, email address, phone number and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored encrypted, and we never see them.
  • Order information: the products or services you order or request, the amount, the order status and any notes about delivery.
  • Pi Network information: when you pay with Pi, we receive from Pi Network your Pi username, a payment identifier and the blockchain transaction ID. We do not receive or store your wallet passphrase, private keys or wallet balance.
  • Messages: the name, email, phone and message you send through our Contact form.
  • Technical information: basic data that your browser sends automatically (such as IP address and browser type) to our hosting providers so the website can work. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores your language choice and login session in your browser.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To process your orders and Pi payments, and to show you their status.
  • To contact you about your orders, deliveries and service requests.
  • To answer your messages and questions.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent fraud.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations in Rwanda.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Pi Network, to create, approve and complete your Pi payments.
  • Supabase (database, login and file storage) and GitHub Pages (website hosting): service providers that store and serve our data on our behalf. Their servers may be located outside Rwanda.
  • Delivery or customs partners, only the details needed to deliver or import a vehicle you ordered.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. We keep order and payment records for as long as Rwandan accounting and tax law requires. Contact messages are deleted once they are no longer needed. Pi blockchain transactions are public and permanent by nature and cannot be deleted by us.

6. Your rights

In line with Rwanda's Law N° 058/2021 on the protection of personal data and privacy, you may ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can also edit your name and phone in My account);
  • delete your account and data, except records we must keep by law;
  • stop or limit certain uses of your data.

To make a request, email aysah22n@gmail.com from the email address of your account. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords and access rules that let each client see only their own orders. Payment approval is handled by our secured server, and our Pi API key is never exposed in the website. No system is 100% secure, so please keep your password private and never share your Pi wallet passphrase with anyone, including us.

8. Children

Our services are intended for adults (18 years or older). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
